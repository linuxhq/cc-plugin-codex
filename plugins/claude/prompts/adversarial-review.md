Perform a design challenge in addition to the correctness review. Examine the
assumptions the implementation depends on, the behavior under failures and
concurrency, recovery paths, and whether a simpler approach would meet the same
requirements. Follow the user's focus when provided.

Separate demonstrated defects from design questions. For design concerns,
explain the concrete tradeoff and evidence that would settle it; do not present
speculative alternatives as proven bugs. Keep the review grounded in the actual
change.
